# Guma One — Content and Market Positioning Revision

## Editorial objective
Present a credible seed-stage venture portfolio: clear customer problems, realistic business models, differentiated product roles, and a phased shared-platform strategy. No fabricated traction, revenue, customer counts, investor commitments, or completed integrations.

## Portfolio logic
- **Commerce infrastructure:** Guma Kart (social commerce checkout), Veyron POS (in-store sales), GumaPrint (print operations).
- **Demand marketplaces:** Kuya Eddie (services/OFW support), PrintAir (print sourcing), GUMALO (general shopping), REKTA (D2C).
- **Vertical supply chains:** Seafoods GO (seafood), FARMMER (agriculture), SOLARekta (solar advisory and installer matching).
- **Fulfillment:** BayanGo (local and rural delivery).
- **Shared enablement, proposed:** GUMA ABIOS, GumaDial, GUMAweb, GUMA AI Studio.

## Investor framing
1. Start with a specific customer and underserved workflow.
2. Explain who pays and why: software subscriptions, lead fees, transaction commissions or fulfillment fees depending on product.
3. Separate live/beta products from concepts and integrations on the roadmap.
4. Validate conversion, retention, contribution margin and partner reliability before expanding.
5. Do not imply shared wallet, universal login, automatic fulfillment or AI autonomy is fully deployed.

## Verification required before publication

- Verify any founder experience claims and the investor-facing company/entity name (existing site mixes SGG and Guma One).
- Confirm that all photographs and alt text accurately represent the associated product.
- Obtain legal review for investment language, regulatory products and any performance claims.
- Confirm whether GUMALO and SOLARekta should be publicly listed as coming-soon products.
